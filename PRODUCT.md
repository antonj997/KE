# Elektrikerappen – första konceptet

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

Phone-first is confirmed by the user. Web is a prototype medium, not a decision about the final app platform.

## Users
A self-employed electrician starting a small Swedish electrical business.

## Product Purpose
Manage customers, plan jobs, record working time and material consumption, and turn those records into invoices. The first deliverable is a few mockup screens and a user flow, not a production app.

## Operating Context
Mainly used on a phone. The home screen must make pressing work easy to see. Swedish UI language. Customer → job → time and materials → invoice is the core journey.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Customers, scheduling, time tracking, materials, and invoice preparation are confirmed needs. In this prototype, data and actions are illustrative. Actual sending, accounting, payment synchronization, and tax applications are undecided.

## Brand Commitments
Minimal and calm, showing essential information on each screen. Seven supplied visual references favor readable typography, soft neutral surfaces, rounded controls, and restrained accents. The confirmed company name is Kragsta Elektriska. The supplied existing logo uses condensed slab-serif capitals, orange, and dark green. The chosen app symbol is the Skylt K/lightning silhouette, with stem serifs, orange fill, dark green outline and offset, and the top serif removed from its right arm. It replaces the wordmark in the app and review headers. The full wordmark remains available for invoice documents.

The running-job indicator shows elapsed time throughout the app. The latest user request replaces the floating overlay: the job detail has an inline timer in its scrolling content; all other app screens show a compact, pill-shaped status element at the top right, alongside the K logo. The header reserves its own space above the scrolling content.

Logo selection: the user chose the Skylt serif variant and requested removal of the horizontal serif on its upper right arm. The selected source is `assets/logo-proposals/kragsta-kraft-skylt-ren.svg`; the active copy is `assets/kragsta-icon.svg`.

The user has explicitly selected the tall, tightly spaced, arched full logo shown in codex-clipboard-7cf70036-1080-4ee1-b8bd-19b4ff6ef79e.png as the final company logo for this app. This is the Bernard MT Condensed version generated by design/build-tight-logo.ps1, not the later broad Rockwell variant. Use the selected orange/dark-green stacked wordmark with its integrated K/lightning and orange telephone line. The invoice preview uses the matching version without the telephone number. The previously approved small app symbol remains the companion mark.

## Evidence on Hand
User brief and seven attached visual references. No existing application, production data, identity, or approved technical stack.

## Product Principles
- Make the next action clear.
- Record work once and reuse it for invoicing.
- Show advanced financial details only when relevant.
- Keep app titles free of descriptive taglines or decorative subtitles, as requested by the user. Operational date and job context may remain.
- Keep statuses readable without relying on color.

## Open Decisions
Final platform and implementation stack; invoicing/accounting integrations; offline persistence; pricing and tax edge cases. Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript is the author's temporary choice for this reviewable prototype only.
