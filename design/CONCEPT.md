# First phone concept

This is an explicitly requested exploratory mockup, not a production implementation. Six screens and a linked flow are the reviewable deliverable. The user's seven images and calm/minimal direction take precedence over aesthetic randomization.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A working day, with a clear next action. Borrow the calm hierarchy of a Swedish wall calendar rather than a financial dashboard full of charts.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper (#f7f7f2), ink (#252923), muted lemon (#e9eca5), sage (#e5ebdf), and quiet rules (#dddfd5). Segoe UI is a deliberate workhorse face for phone operation. Large regular headings, soft 16px surfaces, and touch targets at least 44px. Black primary actions.

STORY: Understand what needs attention, find the customer, plan a job, record the actual work, review the invoice, then track payment and any ROT application.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A two-column task summary, one large next-visit panel, and two explicit attention rows. Six phone canvases sit on a neutral review board; the focused prototype shows one phone at a time. No decorative charts or fictional business claims. All content is demo data.

FORM: Candidate 6, Swedish wall calendar translated into a working day. Seed 0db7ed69 ran in degraded mode without catalog challengers. Other grounded forms considered: service notebook, work-order pad, instrument display, address book, weekly planner, and invoice folio. The supplied aesthetic and mobile task hierarchy constrain all of them.

FINISH: Review the six screens at desktop and phone widths, verify the linked customer-to-invoice demo, and document the actual visual tokens. This mockup does not select a final technical stack or claim invoice compliance.

## Interaction
One main action per task. The job detail uses an inline timer in the scrolling content. Other screens show a compact, pill-shaped status element at the top right, with the K logo kept clear on the left. The header reserves space above the scrolling content. It identifies the current job, updates elapsed session time, opens the job, and stops/saves the session. The active home screen shows the next visit compactly so the attention rows still have space. Time and material totals feed the invoice. The invoice header places Faktura on the left and the company logo on the right. Invoice sending is labeled simulation and never contacts an external service. ROT defaults off; a separate review gate collects illustrative property and applicant details before enabling a ROT preview.

## Confirmed branding update
The user confirmed the company name as Kragsta Elektriska and supplied its existing orange/dark-green, condensed slab-serif logo. They selected the Skylt K/lightning variant, with its upper right serif removed. The orange symbol has a dark green outline and offset and retains serifs on its stem. It is shown at 36px in the app and 40px in the review header. The full SVG wordmark is retained for invoices.
